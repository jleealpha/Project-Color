What is Project Color?

Project Color is a web-based color vision screening tool designed to help you understand your color vision in detail. Unlike simple yes/no tests, we provide a specific diagnosis of your color vision type.

Comprehensive Testing

We combine two validated testing methods to assess your color vision across multiple dimensions.

Personalized Results

Receive a detailed classification of your color vision, from normal trichromacy to specific types of color vision deficiencies.

No Account Needed

Complete the test immediately without creating an account. Your results are generated in-session and ready to download.
